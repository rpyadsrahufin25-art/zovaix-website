Before we talk

THE USEFUL QUESTIONS.

We prefer a good-fit conversation to a hard sell. Here is what people usually ask before they contact us.

Do you only run Meta ads?

Meta ads are the entry point here. When it helps the outcome, ZOVAIX can also improve the landing page, tracking, CRM handoff, Google Ads and creative process around the campaign.

Can you work with an existing ad account?

Yes. We begin by understanding what is already in place, what is useful to preserve and what should change before new money is spent.

What happens after I request an audit?

We reply on WhatsApp, learn the business context and share the most useful next step. If we are a fit, we can then plan a proper campaign and conversion system.

Do you promise a specific number of leads or sales?

No honest team can guarantee a result without understanding your market, offer, sales process and budget. We promise a clear process, direct communication and decisions rooted in evidence.
