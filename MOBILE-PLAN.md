# ZOVAIX mobile — visual rebuild plan

## Principle

Mobile must not load or play either desktop scroll film. It will use layered CSS/SVG 3D objects, transform-only animation, tiny vector marks and real HTML content—fast on an average Android phone.

## Creative idea: The growth engine

Desktop is cinematic; mobile is tactile. A visitor enters a living growth engine, sees how each component works, understands the offer, then starts a conversation. It is not a compressed desktop page.

## Storyboard

1. **Hero / first four seconds** — dark emerald space, a floating glass ZOVAIX core with rotating rings and an orbiting signal; header has wordmark plus **Book a call**; headline, **Start a project**, **See the engine**, and quiet WhatsApp access.
2. **Proof of thinking** — editorial cream chapter, “One team. Everything in motion.”, plain-language outcome copy, five service markers, and a lightweight platform ticker.
3. **3D service engine** — five full-width scenes: Ads, Social, Automation, Websites, Media. Each has a distinct colour world, useful 3D miniature and **Talk about this** action.
4. **How the system connects** — Attract → Convert → Scale pathway that explains the hand-off from traffic, through response, to sales and measurement, ending in **Build my engine**.
5. **See it in action** — swipeable CSS product demos for instant WhatsApp replies, content publishing, ad testing and website lead capture. Each names the business outcome.
6. **Dashboard / proof** — clean light chapter with a clearly-labelled sample dashboard for leads, replies, booked calls and campaign learning; no invented client results.
7. **Founder trust** — supplied founder portrait, personal promise, response-time reassurance, **Book a call** and **WhatsApp Roshan**.
8. **Start a project** — three-step brief: goal, business name, reply channel. It creates a pre-filled WhatsApp message instead of pretending to submit a backend form.
9. **Footer** — contact email, social links, services, legal links and return to top.

## Performance and UX rules

- Animate only transform and opacity; pause nonessential motion off-screen.
- Respect reduced motion and safe areas; all targets are at least 44px.
- No video, canvas, WebGL, Lottie, third-party tracker or mobile image-frame preload.
- WhatsApp appears intentionally: hero/header, after the hero, service actions and the final brief.

## Success test

Within one screen, a visitor understands that ZOVAIX connects ads, content, automation and websites. At every meaningful decision point, one thumb action starts a real conversation.
